刺繡祭服的神聖性The Sacredness of Embroidery Costumes

繡在針線間的敬天法祖A Faith Sewn in Thread

西南民族視刺繡盛裝為神聖之物——它在祖先與神靈的指引下製成，被視為家庭的祝福。每次使用後都以皮革仔細包裹、妥善收藏，因此我們常能在村寨中，見到六、七代前傳下、仍完好如初的繡衣。

The peoples of southwest China regard embroidered costumes as sacred — made under the guidance of spirits and ancestors, and seen as a blessing upon the family. After each use it is wrapped in leather and carefully kept, which is why, collecting in the villages, we find costumes handed down six or seven generations still in excellent condition.

繡片的主題也帶著靈性：所有紋樣皆與現實社會無關，映照的是民族的精神世界與宇宙觀。他們相信祖先即是神明、神明即是祖先——刺繡是為指引他們的祖先而作，因此虔敬地追求極致。這也解釋了為何一位繡娘願意花數年，只為完成一件衣裳。

The themes are spiritual too: none of the patterns narrate real life; they reflect the people's spiritual world and their view of the cosmos. They believed their ancestors were divinities and their gods were ancestors — the embroidery is made for the ancestors who guide it, pursued with piety and excellence. This explains why a woman will spend several years on a single costume.

龍The Dragon

龍是西南民族刺繡中最尊貴的主題——蟠龍、雙頭龍、人首龍、太極陰陽龍，紋樣千變萬化，承載著溝通天地的神力。

The dragon is the most exalted theme in southwestern embroidery — coiled dragons, twin-headed dragons, human-headed dragons, the taiji yin-yang dragon — endlessly varied, carrying the sacred power that links heaven and earth.

虎神The Tiger God

虎是威猛與守護的象徵，對應西方白虎之神。在繡片與蠟染中，虎神鎮邪驅祟，護佑族人與家園。

The tiger is a symbol of might and protection, answering to the White Tiger god of the west. In embroidery and batik, the Tiger God wards off evil and guards the people and their homeland.

蝴蝶媽媽The Mother of Butterflies

在苗族古歌中，蝴蝶媽媽是萬物與人類的始祖。她的形象化為繡片與背帶上的紋樣，寄託著生命起源與繁衍的信仰。

In Miao ancient songs, the Butterfly Mother is the primal ancestor of all things and of humankind. Her image becomes the motif on embroidered panels and baby-carriers, carrying a faith in the origin and continuance of life.

眾神The Gods

從瑤族師公的法事、馬王堆帛衣，到布依族背帶上的「一團和氣」、黎族的創世大力神與天機神——西南民族把眾神穿在身上，祈求上天的護佑。

From the rituals of Yao priests and the silk robes of Mawangdui, to the "harmony" figure on Buyi baby-carriers and the Li creation-god and heaven-loom god — the southwestern peoples wear their gods upon them, praying for the protection of Heaven.
