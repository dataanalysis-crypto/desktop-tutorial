Guzang Festival · Sacrificial Robe

鼓藏祭祀服 · 百鳥衣The Hundred-Birds Coat

苗族祭祖主祭服Guzang Sacrificial Robe of the Miao

鼓藏節是苗族最古老、也最重要而獨特的節日。關於「鼓」與「藏」二字的由來眾說紛紜——無論何種源流，它始終是苗族文化中最隆重的節日之一。

The Guzang (Drum-Zang) Festival is the oldest — and among the most important — of all Miao festivals. Whatever the origin of its name, it has always been among the most solemn festivals of Miao culture.

十三年一度的祭典A Rite Once Every Thirteen Years

鼓藏節是夏朝苗族的祭祀節日，每 13 年舉行一次。相傳姑娘彭巧為尋找救命恩人寶庚，召集蘆笙與鼓，讓人們聚在一起唱歌跳舞；歷經十二年，二人才終成眷屬。無論起源為何，這個節日都是為了慶祝豐收、紀念祖先。

The festival was held once every thirteen years. Legend tells of the girl Peng Qiao, who gathered pipes and drums to find her rescuer Bao Geng; after twelve years the two were finally wed. Whatever its origin, the festival celebrates the harvest and commemorates the ancestors.

「嘎鬧」，即鳥的部族之意——他們是上古蚩尤集團中以鳥為圖騰的「羽族」後裔。"Ga-Nao" — the people of the birds; descendants of the feathered clan of ancient Chiyou who took the bird as their totem.

百鳥衣The Hundred-Birds Coat

百鳥衣是鼓藏節的主祭服，其旗幡以長幅蠟染布與織錦製成，滿佈鳥紋，並綴以沾滿白色鳥羽的祭祀樹。主要流傳於黔東南丹寨、雷山、榕江、三都一帶。

The Hundred-Birds coat is the principal robe of the Guzang festival; its banners are made of long strips of batik and brocade, densely covered with bird motifs and hung with sacrificial trees of white feathers. It survives mainly around Danzhai, Leishan, Rongjiang and Sandu in southeastern Guizhou.

唐貞觀三年，苗族首領謝元琛身著「百鳥衣」入朝參見唐太宗，太宗命畫家閻立德臨摹為「王會圖」——這是百鳥衣較早見於文獻的記載。

In the Tang dynasty the Miao chieftain Xie Yuanchen came to court in a Hundred-Birds coat; Emperor Taizong had the painter Yan Lide copy it in the "Wang-Hui Tu" — the earliest documentary record of the coat.

蟠龍紋 · 源自先秦的古老圖騰The Coiled-Dragon Motif

蟠龍紋多見於先秦，以殷商最為顯著：龍身以龍首為中心蟠卷成環。自西周以降漸淡出歷史，唯獨西南少數民族保持著先秦的圖像特徵，三千多年傳承至今。

The coiled dragon appears mostly in pre-Qin times, most distinctly in the Yin-Shang: the body coils around the head into a ring. It faded after the Western Zhou — only the southwestern peoples kept the pre-Qin imagery, handing it down for over three thousand years.
