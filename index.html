眾神在服飾之上The Gods on Clothing

中國西南的大多數民族相信，人類是宇宙不可分割的一部分——這一宇宙觀可追溯至數千年前的古老中華文明。他們相信神明的存在，並藉由穿著傳統服飾表達信仰。

Most of the ethnic peoples of southwest China believe that we humans are an integral part of the cosmos — a worldview traceable to ancient Chinese civilization thousands of years ago. They express their faith by wearing their traditional garments in modern society.

彝族將神秘的河圖洛書原始圖案繡於衣上；苗族則在祭天敬神的儀式中，穿上繡滿神祇的衣裳，維繫著人與自然的和諧。

The Yi embroider the primordial patterns of the mystical He-tu and Luo-shu; the Miao dress in garments embroidered with deities when worshipping heaven, maintaining harmony between Man and Nature.

薩滿之路The Path of the Shaman

「薩滿」saman 一詞源自滿語及通古斯語族，意為「智者」、「曉徹」，指有能力進入入神狀態、並能與神溝通之人。

The word "shaman" (saman) comes from Manchu and other Tungusic languages, meaning "one who knows" — a person able to enter a trance state and to commune with the gods.

在中國西南，各民族都有不同的薩滿——他們是神靈世界的守護者，也是現實世界中最後的通靈人。

Across southwest China, every people has its own shamans — the guardians of the spirit world, and the last mediums of the living one.

復活的史前文明A Civilization Reborn in Thread

天工織物，緣於一份對中華神傳文化的敬意與眷戀。我們收藏、記錄並研究中國西南少數民族的手工服飾——那是尚在人間流傳的、來自遙遠上一輪文明的活化石。

The Art of Handwork was born of reverence and longing for China's divinely inspired culture. We collect, document, and study the handmade costumes of the ethnic peoples of southwest China — living fossils of a distant, earlier civilization still passed down among us.
