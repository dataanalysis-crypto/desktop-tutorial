Jiaxie · Clamp-resist Dye

夾纈Jiaxie

雕版鏤空的防染古藝An Ancient Clamp-Resist Dye

夾纈（xié），現代也稱夾染，是一種傳統雕版鏤空印花防染工藝，也是中國最古老的印染工藝之一。

Jiaxie (also called clamp-dyeing today) is a traditional carved-block, openwork resist-dyeing craft — one of the oldest printing-and-dyeing arts of China.

兩板夾染Dyed Between Two Blocks

夾纈是用夾版入染的工藝：兩塊刻有花紋、互相吻合的花板夾住織物染色；染液無法滲入凸起的花紋，卻能在凹陷處著色，於是白花藍地、圖案兩面對稱。

Jiaxie dyes cloth clamped between blocks: two carved boards that mirror each other press the fabric; dye cannot reach the raised pattern but sinks into the hollows, leaving white figures on an indigo ground, symmetrical on both faces.

盛於唐宋Splendour of the Tang and Song

夾纈始於秦漢，盛於唐宋。隋煬帝曾令工匠印染五彩夾纈花羅裙賞賜宮女百官；唐代色彩斑斕、極為盛行，甚至作為「國禮」贈予各國遣唐使——日本正倉院至今珍藏著當年帶回的彩色夾纈，在日本被稱為「板締」。

Jiaxie began in the Qin-Han and flourished in the Tang and Song. Emperor Yang of Sui had five-coloured jiaxie skirts made as gifts for court ladies and officials; in the Tang it was dazzlingly popular, even given as a "state gift" to foreign envoys — the coloured jiaxie they carried home is still treasured in Japan's Shōsōin, where it is called itajime.

浙南藍夾纈The Blue Jiaxie of Southern Zhejiang

後來中國的夾纈轉向單色的藍夾纈，在浙南民間用作婚嫁的被面。木版多以紅柴、棠梨、楊梅、楓木製作，圖案以晚清以來流行的戲曲為主。溫州完整保存了雕版、製靛、印染的工藝流程；1997年台灣漢聲雜誌對藍夾纈進行田野調查並出版專輯，首次向外界宣告這門工藝依然活態存在。

In time Chinese jiaxie turned to single-colour blue, used in southern Zhejiang as wedding quilt-covers. The blocks were carved from redwood, birchleaf pear, bayberry and maple, their designs drawn from late-Qing opera. Wenzhou preserves the full process of carving, indigo-making and dyeing; in 1997 Taiwan's Echo magazine surveyed the craft and published a volume, announcing to the world that it was still a living art.
