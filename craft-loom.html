The Back-strap Loom · Hemudu

踞織機The Back-strap Loom

六千年前的織機A Loom Six Thousand Years Old

1975年，浙江餘姚河姆渡新石器時代遺址出土的原始織布工具——踞機（腰機），距今六千多年，是目前迄今發現世界上最早的原始織布工具。

In 1975, a primitive weaving tool — the back-strap loom — was unearthed at the Neolithic Hemudu site in Yuyao, Zhejiang. Over six thousand years old, it is the earliest known weaving tool in the world.

最古老的織機The Oldest Loom

海南黎族至今仍用這種古老的腰機織布。《尚書·禹貢》有「島夷卉服，厥篚織貝」之記；西漢時海南的「廣幅布」被徵作歲貢。宋末元初，黃道婆向黎族學習紡織，總結出「錯紗、配色、綜線、挈花」之法，返鄉後大大推動了長江流域的棉紡織業。

The Li people of Hainan still weave on this ancient loom today. The Book of Documents records "the island tribes in grass clothes, their baskets of woven shell-cloth"; in the Western Han, Hainan's "broad cloth" was levied as tribute. In the late Song, Huang Daopo learned weaving from the Li and codified the arts of "crossing yarns, matching colours, healds and pattern-lifting," transforming cotton weaving across the Yangtze on her return.

席地而織Woven on the Ground

踞織機又稱腰機，由經軸、分經杆、布軸、打緯刀、背帶與綜杆等組成，沒有固定機架——以人代之，用腰帶縛在織者腰上。織工席地而坐，靠雙腳與腰脊控制經線張力；藉分經棍分出梭口，一梭一梭地織就。

The back-strap loom has a warp beam, shed rod, cloth beam, beating knife, back-strap and heald — but no fixed frame. The weaver's own body is the frame: a strap around the waist holds the warp taut as she sits on the ground, controlling tension with feet and spine, opening the shed with a rod and weaving pick by pick.

黎錦Li Brocade

雲南晉寧石寨山出土的青銅貯貝器蓋上，鑄有十八名女性踞坐而織的場面，生動記錄了漢代的紡織生產。黎錦的花紋多由手工挑花而成，形成人紋、魚紋、蛙紋等幾何圖案；圖中常見神話傳說中守護海南的大力神。

On the lid of a bronze cowrie container from Shizhaishan sit eighteen cast female figures weaving — a vivid record of Han textile work. Li brocade patterns are picked by hand into geometric human, fish and frog motifs; among them recurs the mighty guardian god of Hainan legend.
