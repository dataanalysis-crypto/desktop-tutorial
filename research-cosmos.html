服飾上的宇宙密碼Secret Codes of the Cosmos

繡在衣上的宇宙符號Cosmic Symbols Woven onto Cloth

中國西南的大多數民族相信，人類是宇宙不可分割的一部分。他們把神授的圖案繡於衣上——那是宇宙結構的全息圖，是被視為神授、傳承自上一輪文明的密碼。

Most ethnic peoples of southwest China believe humans are an integral part of the cosmos. They embroider divinely given patterns onto their clothes — holograms of the structure of the universe, secret codes handed down from before this civilization.

螺旋 · 回家的路The Spiral — the Way Home

從銀河到指紋，螺旋是世界史前遺存中分布最廣、最易辨認的符號之一。在中國甲骨文中，螺旋意為「回」。西南民族把「回家的路」繡在衣上——他們相信，真我被創造之處，就在星辰之間，那才是真正的故鄉；穿上這符號，是為了提醒自己回家的路。

From the Milky Way to our fingerprints, the spiral is among the most widespread symbols in prehistoric relics worldwide. In Chinese oracle-bone script it means "to return." The southwestern peoples embroidered "the way home" onto their clothes — believing that where their true self was created lies among the stars, their real homeland; they wear the symbol to remind themselves of the way back.

卍 · 吉祥的萬德The Srivatsa

早在納粹挪用之前，「卍」在世界各地已有悠久歷史，從歐洲、非洲到亞洲，可溯至數千年前的青銅與鐵器時代——已知最早的一件出土於烏克蘭 Mezine，刻於一萬餘年前的象牙雕像上。「卍」是佛家吉祥之相，唐代玄奘譯為「德」；梵語意為「集一切吉祥」。其四臂向外延展，可衍生出象徵長壽與福祿的種種織錦紋樣。

Long before the Nazis hijacked it, the swastika had a long history worldwide — from Europe to Africa to Asia, back thousands of years to the Bronze and Iron Ages; the earliest known example was found at Mezine, Ukraine, carved on an ivory figurine over ten thousand years old. In the Buddhist tradition it is srivatsa, rendered "mighty virtue" by Xuanzang in the seventh century; in Sanskrit it means "the gathering of all good fortune." Its four arms extend outward into countless brocade patterns of long life and blessing.

陰陽 · 天之道Yin and Yang — the Way of Heaven

陰陽象徵中國傳統哲學中兩者相生相剋的關係。老子《道德經》云：「道生一，一生二，二生三，三生萬物。萬物負陰而抱陽，沖氣以為和。」《淮南子·天文訓》述天地起源：清者上升為天（陽），濁者凝結為地（陰），二者交感而生萬物。所謂「道」，正是陰陽變化之律、天地運行之法——人須順天而行，方得長久。

Yin and Yang embody the mutually generating and restraining relationship of two things in Chinese philosophy. Laozi wrote: "Tao produces one, one produces two (yin and yang), two produce three, and three produce all things; all things carry yin and embrace yang." The Huainanzi describes creation: the light rose as heaven (yang), the heavy settled as earth (yin), and their interaction bore all things. The Tao is the law by which yin and yang change — the Way of Heaven, which we must follow to flourish.

八芒星 · 八卦與方位The Octagram — the Eight Trigrams

八芒星是又一個橫跨古文明的符號：從蘇美爾壁畫，到凌家灘遺址的玉版、大汶口陶器，再到《周易》八卦與彝族的八卦方位圖——它在西南民族的織錦與刺繡中反覆出現，成為連結天地方位的宇宙圖式。

The octagram is another symbol spanning ancient civilizations — from Sumerian murals to the jade plaque of Lingjiatan and Dawenkou pottery, to the Eight Trigrams of the Book of Changes and the Yi people's trigram compass. It recurs throughout the brocade and embroidery of the southwest as a cosmic diagram binding the directions of heaven and earth.
