Tiger-Head Hat · White Tiger

虎頭帽The Tiger-Head Hat

人文與天文的對應Between the Human and the Heavens

虎頭帽是中國傳統民俗文化中的一種童帽，流傳地區從山東、山西、陝西、甘肅延伸至四川、雲南——這正與四象之一白虎七宿的分野地區一致。

The tiger-head hat is a child's cap of Chinese folk tradition, found from Shandong, Shanxi, Shaanxi and Gansu to Sichuan and Yunnan — precisely the region mapped to the White Tiger's seven mansions among the Four Symbols.

來歷Origins

虎頭帽通常冬季使用，外形模仿虎頭：圓形帽頂、後綴虎尾狀穗子，常配鈴鐺與小帶，既保暖又具裝飾。據文物記載，其歷史可追溯至唐代——西安收藏的一件唐代虎頭帽襁褓陶俑，被認為是目前已知最早的實物證據。

Worn mostly in winter, the hat mimics a tiger's head: a round crown, a tail-like tassel behind, often with bells and small straps — warm and decorative at once. Records trace it to the Tang dynasty; a Tang pottery figurine of a swaddled infant in a tiger-head hat, held in Xi'an, is thought to be the earliest known physical evidence.

虎頭力士The Tiger-Headed Guardian

漢代以來的墓葬藝術與寺廟裝飾中，常見「虎頭力士」——虎頭人身，出現於墓門或神龕兩側，象徵力量與守護。南陽出土的「白虎星座畫像石」刻畫昂首翹尾的白虎伴隨星辰，顯示虎的形象與天文崇拜的結合。

From the Han onward, tomb art and temple decoration often show the "tiger-headed guardian" — tiger-headed, human-bodied, standing at tomb doors or shrine sides as a symbol of power and protection. A Han stone relief of the White Tiger constellation from Nanyang shows the beast amid the stars, uniting the tiger with astronomical worship.

人文與天文的對應Between the Human and the Heavens

老虎在中國文化中是威猛、辟邪、驅鬼的象徵。《易經》云「風從虎」，《風俗通義》載「虎者陽物，百獸之長……噬食鬼魅」。而凡間之虎，對應著天庭的白虎星宿。

In Chinese culture the tiger is a symbol of might that wards off evil. The Book of Changes says "the wind follows the tiger"; the Fengsu Tongyi calls it "a creature of yang, chief of beasts… devourer of demons." The earthly tiger answers to the heavenly White Tiger.

「白虎」是四象之一，代表西方，對應二十八宿中的西方七宿：奎、婁、胃、昴、畢、觜、參。透過「分野」，天上的星宿與地上的州郡一一對應——而虎頭帽流傳的地區，恰與白虎七宿的分野相合。

The White Tiger, one of the Four Symbols, represents the west and its seven lunar mansions: Kui, Lou, Wei, Mao, Bi, Zi and Shen. Through the theory of "field-allocation," heaven's mansions map to earthly regions — and the range of the tiger-head hat matches exactly the allotment of the White Tiger's seven mansions.
