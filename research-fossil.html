復活的史前文明The Living Fossil of Prehistoric Civilization

一部無字的史詩An Epic Without Words

若以針為筆、以線為墨，西南民族早已把自己的歷史畫在衣上。兩千年前的大遷徙中，文字或許散佚，但衣上的古老符號千年相隨，成為民族的靈魂。西南民族的服飾，是一部無字的史詩。

If we take needles as pens and threads as ink, the peoples of southwest China have drawn their history on their clothes. In the great migration two thousand years ago, written texts may have been lost — but the ancient symbols on their garments stayed with them for millennia, the soul of the people. Their clothing is an epic without words.

苗族把祖先遷徙的路線繡在裙上；納西族把星辰與日月穿在身上，那是古老的宇宙星圖；瑤族把神明穿在身上，祈求上天的護佑。封存在紋樣與符號中的歷史記憶，記錄著人類的起源與來自宇宙的訊息。

The Miao record their ancestors' migration routes on their skirts; the Naxi wear stars and moons — an ancient star map; the Yao wear their gods, praying for Heaven's blessing. Sealed within the patterns are memories that record the origin of humankind and messages from the cosmos.

彝曆The Yi Calendar

彝族源自古羌、西羌部族。作為中國最古老的部族之一，羌人居於西部崑崙山一帶——黃帝、炎帝、少昊、禹等上古帝王多由此興起（見《史記》）。今日的羌、彝、納西、傈僳、哈尼、土家、白、藏等族，皆為古羌人的後裔。彝族至今仍在使用古老的曆法：十月太陽曆、十二月陰陽曆與十八月太陽曆。

The Yi descend from the ancient Qiang. As one of China's oldest peoples, the Qiang lived in the Kunlun mountains of the west, whence many ancient emperors — Huangdi, Yandi, Shaohao, Yu — first rose (see the Records of the Grand Historian). The modern Qiang, Yi, Naxi, Lisu, Hani, Tujia, Bai and Tibetans are all their descendants. The Yi still use ancient calendars: the ten-month solar, the twelve-month yin-yang, and the eighteen-month solar.

十二月陰陽曆The Twelve-Month Yin-Yang Calendar

這是彝族最常用、自史前傳承的曆法。一年分五季（春、夏、長夏、秋、冬），對應木、火、土、銅、水五行；每季七十二天，再分公母兩月。年、月、日皆以十二生肖表示——這一習俗至今在中國十分流行。

This is the Yi people's most common calendar, handed down from prehistory. A year has five seasons (spring, summer, long summer, autumn, winter) matching the five elements — wood, fire, earth, copper, water; each 72-day season is split into a male and a female month. Years, months and days are all named after the twelve animals — a custom still popular across China.

十八月太陽曆 · 與馬雅的巧合The Eighteen-Month Solar Calendar

世上公認的十八月太陽曆，只有南美馬雅人使用了六千餘年。巧合的是，1989 年中國社科院學者在雲南武定縣發現，當地占星者使用著與馬雅完全相同的十八月太陽曆：一年十八個月、每月二十天，共三百六十天；唯一不同的是餘下的五天——馬雅稱之為「禁忌日」，彝族稱之為「過年日」。

The only recognized eighteen-month solar calendar was used by the Maya of South America for over six thousand years. Remarkably, in 1989 scholars of the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences found that astrologers in Wuding County, Yunnan used the very same calendar: eighteen months of twenty days, 360 in all — differing only in the last five days, which the Maya called "taboo days" and the Yi called "New Year days."

古老的節日Ancient Festivals

彝族火把節，又稱「星回節」，是雲南彝、白、納西、基諾、拉祜等族的重要節日，多於農曆六月二十五舉行——有學者認為它原是十月太陽曆的過年日，是上半年的「大年」。苗族的姊妹節則於農曆三月十五至十八舉行，婦女盛裝、圍成巨大的螺旋圈載歌載舞——這是古老的祭祀儀式，也是「天人合一」宇宙觀的體現。

The Yi Torch Festival, also called the "Star-Returning Festival," is a major feast of the Yi, Bai, Naxi, Jinuo and Lahu of Yunnan, held around the 25th of the sixth lunar month — thought by some to have been the New Year of the ten-month solar calendar, the "Grand New Year" of the first half-year. The Miao Sisters' Day, from the 15th to 18th of the third lunar month, sees women in splendid dress singing and dancing in a great spiral — an ancient rite, and an embodiment of the cosmology of "harmony between heaven and man."
