Sisters' Festival · Shidong

姊妹節Sisters' Festival

全銀盛裝盛宴 · 亞洲最古老的情人節A Silver Feast — Asia's Oldest Valentine's Day

2004年，我站在清水河畔，望著頭戴銀鳳冠、身著五彩衣裳的苗族婦女，在低沉的銅鼓聲中吟誦古歌、搖曳起舞，恍惚間穿越到屈原《九歌》所繪的東皇太一祭祀場面。

In 2004 I stood by the Qingshui River, watching Miao women in silver phoenix crowns and many-coloured robes chant ancient songs and sway to deep bronze drums — as if transported to the sacrificial rites of Qu Yuan's "Nine Songs."

苗人的故事The Hmong's Story

這個古老民族源自五千多年前的九黎部落；首領蚩尤與黃帝戰於涿鹿而敗，中華文明自此分嶺。九黎變三苗、三苗變楚蠻，最後成為苗族；三千多年前開始第五次大遷徙，來到貴州、雲南與湘西定居。

This ancient people descends from the Jiuli tribe of over five thousand years ago. After their leader Chiyou was defeated by the Yellow Emperor at Zhuolu, Chinese civilization divided. Jiuli became Sanmiao, Sanmiao became the Chu peoples, and at last the Miao — who, over three thousand years ago, migrated to settle in Guizhou, Yunnan and western Hunan.

姊妹節The Sisters' Festival

苗族「姊妹節」是慶祝春天與愛情的節日。每年農曆三月，貴州台江施洞的年輕婦女穿上最好的刺繡盛裝、戴上隆重銀飾，去尋找合適的伴侶。兩三天的節日裡，人們跳舞、唱歌、喝米酒、看鬥牛鬥雞與賽馬——這是亞洲最古老的情人節。

The Miao "Sisters' Festival" celebrates spring and love. Each third lunar month, young women of Shidong in Taijiang, Guizhou put on their finest embroidered dress and heavy silver, and go in search of a partner. Over two or three days they dance, sing, drink rice wine and watch bullfights and horse races — Asia's oldest Valentine's Day.

苗族人相信象徵光明的銀能驅邪，也是財富與美麗的象徵；有的姑娘一次戴上好幾公斤銀飾。精緻的銀頭飾、銀項圈與銀鏈在陽光下閃爍，姑娘們自豪地展示著自己親手製作的盛裝。

The Miao believe that silver — the colour of light — wards off evil, and is a token of wealth and beauty; some young women wear several kilograms at once. Delicate silver headdresses, torcs and chains glitter in the sun as the girls proudly display costumes made by their own hands.

施洞盛裝 · 一件衣服的十八般技藝Shidong Splendour

施洞苗族盛裝以精湛的破線繡，將世代相傳的古歌故事化為內容豐富的繡片。破線繡把一根絲線分為 8–16 根極細的絲線，用皂角過漿後於紙樣上刺繡；一件一等盛裝往往需 3–4 年才能完成，是女孩出嫁時重要的資產。

Shidong festive dress uses the exquisite "split-thread" embroidery to turn ancient songs into richly detailed panels. A single silk thread is split into 8–16 hair-fine strands, sized with soap-pod, then embroidered over a paper pattern; a first-rank costume can take 3–4 years to finish, and is a bride's most precious asset.

育藍、織染、繡花、打銀——一套完整的施洞盛裝，需要十八般技藝方能造就。Growing indigo, weaving, dyeing, embroidering, silver-smithing — a complete Shidong costume takes eighteen crafts to make.
