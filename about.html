About the Artisan

關於 · 古貞珍About · Zhen Zhen Gu

母親 · 匠人 · 畫家 · 收藏者Mother · Artisan · Painter · Collector

古貞珍——母親、匠人、畫家、設計者，也是傳統手工織物的收藏者，如今旅居南太平洋的島嶼。二十餘年來，她走遍中國西南，向少數民族匠人習藝，收藏並研究瀕臨失傳的手工服飾與生產工具。

Zhen Zhen Gu — mother, artisan, painter, designer, and collector of traditional handmade textiles, now living on an island in the South Pacific. For over two decades she has travelled across southwest China, learning from ethnic artisans and collecting the handmade costumes and tools now at risk of vanishing.

歷程A Life in Craft

1979

生於中國河北石家莊。

Born in Shijiazhuang, Hebei Province, China.

1998–2002

烏克蘭哈爾科夫國立設計與美術學院，油畫系學士。

Kharkiv State Academy of Design and Fine Arts, Ukraine — B.A., Oil Painting.

2002–2004

基輔國立建築大學，造型與裝飾藝術碩士。

M.A. in Modelling & Decorative Arts, Kyiv State University of Architecture.

2004

返回中國，任教於中央美術學院。

Returned to China; became a teacher at the Central Academy of Fine Arts.

2005

深入中國西南，向少數民族匠人學習染、紡、繡等傳統手藝，開始數十年的收藏與研究。

Went deep into southwest China, learning dyeing, spinning and embroidery from ethnic artisans — beginning decades of collecting and research.

2009

多媒體作品《三千世界》參展宋莊文化藝術節（北京）。

Multimedia work “Three Thousand Worlds” shown at the Songzhuang Culture & Art Festival, Beijing.

2014

於「雲上之歌」西南民族非遺展演講個人少數民族收藏（北京）。

Lectured on her personal ethnic-minority collection at the Southwest Intangible Heritage Exhibition, Beijing.

2015

舉家移居紐西蘭。

The family moved to New Zealand.

2017

於紐西蘭創辦銀蕨藝術中心，致力於將傳統藝術帶入社群、推動中華文化的海外交流。

Founded the Silver Fern Art Centre in New Zealand, bringing traditional art into the community and sharing Chinese culture overseas.

2018–2019

於奧克蘭舉辦「中國少數民族服飾展」「中國傳統服飾展」。

Held Chinese ethnic-minority and traditional costume exhibitions in Auckland.

2020–至今

加入紐西蘭手工紡織協會，向平均逾七十歲的當地匠人學習紡、織、氈、編、染，並將多年研究整理成書。

Joined the NZ Handmade Textile Association; learning spinning, weaving, felting, knitting and dyeing from local artisans (avg. over 70), and compiling years of research into a book.
